# Fashol Dotcom chooses upay for payroll disbursement

**2022.06.06 — Daily Sun — Partnership — Entry 10 / 10**

Agro-tech startup Fashol Dotcom chose upay, a mobile financial service provider, to disburse payroll to its growing field agent team.

![Daily Sun article photograph on the Fashol–upay partnership announcement.](../assets/images/news-10-dailysun.jpg)

*Fig. 01 — Daily Sun article photograph on the Fashol–upay partnership announcement.*

## Article body

Agro-tech startup Fashol Dotcom selected upay — United Commercial Bank's mobile financial service — to disburse payroll to its field team in June 2022. At the time, the company employed approximately sixty-five field agents across three districts.

The partnership was positioned as an operational alignment: Fashol's farmer-payment rails already used mobile-money providers (bKash, Nagad, upay), so moving company payroll onto one of the same rails reduced operational complexity.

Daily Sun's reporting noted that Fashol was, at the time, among the earlier Bangladesh agri-tech operators to run full mobile-financial-services payroll for field staff.

— End of article.
