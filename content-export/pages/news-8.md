# Fashol joins GROW Impact Accelerator, cohort five

**2024.07.29 — AgFunder News — Recognition — Entry 08 / 10**

Fashol was selected for the fifth cohort of the AgFunder GROW Impact Accelerator, focused on food-price stabilisation and reducing post-harvest loss.

![AgFunder event photograph.](../assets/images/news-8-agfunder.jpeg)

*Fig. 01 — AgFunder event photograph.*

## Article body

Fashol joined the AgFunder GROW Impact Accelerator's fifth cohort in July 2024, focusing on stabilising food prices and reducing food loss. GROW runs a six-month programme targeted at agri-tech companies operating in emerging markets.

The cohort included companies from Southeast Asia, South Asia, and East Africa. Fashol was selected on the basis of its operating record in climate-vulnerable districts and its direct-procurement supply model.

Fashol's place in the cohort is used for partnership introductions, technical advisory, and — potentially — follow-on investment during a later fundraising round.
