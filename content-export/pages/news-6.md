# DITECH, Fashol join hands to ensure better supply chain of perishables

**2022.08.18 — The Daily Star — Partnership — Entry 06 / 10**

Fashol signed a strategic capital investment agreement with Dekko ISHO Technologies (DITECH) to strengthen the perishable supply chain.

![Warehouse aisles with stored goods, illustrative of cold-chain logistics.](../assets/images/warehouse.jpg)

*Fig. 01 — Warehouse aisles with stored goods, illustrative of cold-chain logistics.*

## Article body

Fashol signed a strategic capital investment agreement with Dekko ISHO Technologies (DITECH) in August 2022 to enhance the perishable supply chain across key districts in the Chittagong division.

The agreement established a joint operational framework for cold-chain distribution in districts where Fashol had recently opened hubs. DITECH's existing network complemented Fashol's field-side operations.

At the time of the announcement, the partnership was positioned as the first strategic corporate investment into Fashol, and preceded the $1M pre-seed round that followed in April 2023.

— End of article.
