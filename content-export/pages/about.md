# Three founders, one chain, built in Bangladesh.

§ About · Founded 2019 · Dhaka  23.75° N / 90.39° E · 138 employees · Private

Fashol is a farm-to-business platform operating out of Dhaka, Singapore, and Dubai. We were founded in **2019** by Sakib Hossain, Mamunur Rashid, and Numair Hussain. Today we move perishable produce from more than **25,000** registered smallholder farmers to buyers across three jurisdictions. This page is the company dossier.

## § 01 — Founders

### Three founders, distinct disciplines.

Fashol was built by three people with different training — engineering, operations, and finance — who agreed that Bangladesh's agricultural chain deserved to be designed, not inherited.

#### 01 — Founder & CEO — Sakib Hossain

![Sakib Hossain, founder and CEO of Fashol.](../assets/images/sakib-hossain.png)

> "I want to deliver potatoes on Mars."

Previously an engineer. Started Fashol at 26 after three years of field work across six districts. Featured by Forbes in 2025.

#### 02 — Co-Founder & COO — Mamunur Rashid

![Mamunur Rashid, co-founder and COO of Fashol.](../assets/images/mamunur-rashid.jpg)

#### 03 — Co-Founder & CFO — Numair Hussain

## § 02 — Timeline

Select operating and capital milestones. Internal records.

## § 03 — Operating principles

### Six principles. Operational, not aspirational.

Written into how we decide, hire, and settle with farmers.

- **P.01 — Farmer first.** Pricing decisions start from what the grower will take home.
- **P.02 — Accountability.** Named owners for each commitment — to farmers, buyers, and investors.
- **P.03 — Sustainability.** Waste reduction is a ledger entry, measured per district, per season.
- **P.04 — Human-centered.** Jogaan is tested with agents and farmers who have never used a smartphone.
- **P.05 — Optimisation.** Data first. First principles next. Opinion last.
- **P.06 — Leadership.** Bangladesh's agricultural infrastructure deserves to be designed, not inherited.
