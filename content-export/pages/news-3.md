# Sakib Hossain — founder feature

**2025.04.30 — Forbes — Feature — Entry 03 / 10**

Forbes profiled Fashol founder Sakib Hossain on direct market access and the economics of perishables in Bangladesh.

![Forbes portrait of Sakib Hossain, founder and CEO of Fashol.](../assets/images/news-3-sakib.jpg)

*Fig. 01 — Forbes portrait of Sakib Hossain, founder and CEO of Fashol.*

## Article body

Sakib Hossain is a Bangladeshi entrepreneur and the founder of Fashol, a tech-enabled agriculture-based company empowering farmers with direct market access and efficient supply-chain solutions. Before founding Fashol in 2019, Sakib spent three years working directly with farmers in six of Bangladesh's agricultural districts.

In the Forbes feature, he discusses the economics that led to the company — that a third of what Bangladesh grows spoils before it is sold, and that the price farmers receive is roughly one-fifth of the price the end consumer pays — and the operational model that Fashol is using to address it.

Forbes also covered the company's expansion beyond Bangladesh: the Singapore office handles regional partnerships and export; the Dubai office covers Gulf buyers. Both remain relatively small compared to the Bangladesh operation.

The piece quotes Sakib:

> I want to deliver potatoes on Mars

— a line that has become, by his own admission, an internal benchmark for ambition at the company.

— End of article.
