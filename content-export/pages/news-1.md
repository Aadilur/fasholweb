# সাকিবের ফসল ডটকমে যুক্ত ১১ হাজার কৃষক

সরাসরি কৃষকের কাছ থেকে কৃষিপণ্য বুঝে নিচ্ছেন ফসল ডটকমের প্রতিষ্ঠাতা সাকিব হোসাইন। সম্প্রতি সাতক্ষীরায় এগারো হাজার নতুন কৃষক ফসল ডটকমে যুক্ত হয়েছেন।

## Article body

Prothom Alo, Bangladesh's largest-circulation Bengali daily, reported in February 2024 that Fashol had registered eleven thousand additional smallholder farmers in the Satkhira district, bringing total onboarded farmers above twenty thousand.

Founder Sakib Hossain travelled personally to the district for the article's accompanying photograph: the cabinet-sized collection book, paper and pen, replaced in 2021 by the Jogaan app. The piece focused on what farmers now earn per kilo after aratdars are removed from the chain.

Satkhira is one of Fashol's earliest districts — the first hub opened there in 2019 — and remains the largest by farmer count and by monthly dispatch tonnage.

— End of article.
