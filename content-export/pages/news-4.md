# e-Commerce Movers Award (eCMA) 2023

**2023.11.09 — The Business Standard — Recognition — Entry 04 / 10**

Fashol was named among recognised e-commerce operators at the 2023 eCMA, organised by the e-Commerce Association of Bangladesh (e-CAB).

![e-Commerce Movers Award ceremony, 2023, Dhaka.](../assets/images/news-4-ecma.png)

*Fig. 01 — e-Commerce Movers Award ceremony, 2023, Dhaka.*

## Article body

The e-Commerce Association of Bangladesh (e-CAB) organised the e-Commerce Movers Award (eCMA) 2023 on Thursday, 9 November 2023. Fashol was recognised alongside a selection of other operators for contribution to the country's digital-commerce sector.

The eCMA is an annual award cycle run by e-CAB to acknowledge operators, platforms, and agencies that have expanded the Bangladesh e-commerce market in specific categories. Fashol was recognised in the agri-tech operator category.

The company accepted the award on 9 November at the Dhaka venue. Founder Sakib Hossain attended in person.
