# Agritech startup Fashol gets $1m pre-seed investment

**2023.04.15 — Dhaka Tribune — Investment — Entry 05 / 10**

Dhaka Tribune's reporting on Fashol's $1 million pre-seed close — building technology to optimise the Bangladesh agricultural supply chain.

![Dhaka Tribune photograph related to the Fashol $1M pre-seed round, April 2023.](../assets/images/news-5-dhakatribune.jpeg)

*Fig. 01 — Dhaka Tribune photograph related to the Fashol $1M pre-seed round, April 2023.*

## Article body

Fashol secured $1 million in pre-seed funding from investors including SOSV, South Asia Tech Partners, and angel investors to build technology optimising the supply chain and connecting farmers to the market.

Dhaka Tribune reported that the round would be used to expand the company's field agent network across additional districts, and to build out the Jogaan mobile application — which is used by company agents to register farmers and record transactions.

At the time, Fashol had approximately twenty-two distribution hubs and was operating in five districts.

— End of article.
