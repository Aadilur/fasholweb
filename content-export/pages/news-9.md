# Cutting out the middleman — farmers & retailers in Bangladesh

**2024.09.03 — Orbit Startups — Feature — Entry 09 / 10**

Orbit Startups profiled Fashol in a long-form piece on the operational model of middleman-removal in Bangladesh agriculture.

![Orbit Startups feature photograph: Fashol farmer and agent at a collection site.](../assets/images/news-9-orbit.png)

*Fig. 01 — Orbit Startups feature photograph: Fashol farmer and agent at a collection site.*

## Article body

In Bangladesh, nearly half of the population relies on agriculture for their livelihoods, with 70% of the country's land used to grow crops. However, a highly fragmented chain of intermediaries — commonly five to seven — sits between a grower and a retailer.

Orbit Startups' feature on Fashol examined the company's operational model: a combination of field agents, cold-chain hubs, quality-grading at intake, and direct buyer matching through software. The piece was published in September 2024 and drew on interviews with founder Sakib Hossain, COO Mamunur Rashid, and a number of registered farmers.

Orbit is a venture-studio partner with an interest in emerging-market platforms; the feature served both as editorial coverage and as an investor update.
